# 0005 — Brand identity: the weave, a lowercase wordmark and one violet point

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Phase:** post-0.4.13 (identity)

## Context

Nexus had no identity of its own. The app shipped three unrelated things: an ad-hoc stroke glyph in `NexusLogo.tsx`, a placeholder PWA tile showing a generic network triangle on near-black, and no favicon, social card or written colour/type rules. Every surface that carried the name guessed, and the install icon did not contain anything that said "Nexus".

The identity also has to survive constraints the product imposes: no webfont downloads (self-hosted installs, offline review), a one-ink version for print and terminals, and an app icon that works from 16 px to 512 px.

## Decisions

### 1. The mark is a braid: two strands woven with real over/under gaps

Two sampled cosine strands on a 32-unit grid, a 3.45-unit round-capped stroke, crossing at two points with an **alternating** over/under: the front strand is cut at the first crossing, the back strand at the second. The gaps are geometry, not a painted background, so the mark sits on any colour.

**Why:** "nexus" means a joining, and the product's real behaviour is many sources woven into one answer; a braid says that without a letterform. It also has a silhouette no competitor uses, and it survives at 16 px because the strands merge into one woven band rather than disappearing.

**Alternatives considered and rejected** (all explored at 16/32/96/176 px on light and dark): an "N" monogram with node terminals, a hub-and-spoke graph, a lens/aperture, an orb, a card stack, a portal arch, a marker underline, a four-point spark, a prism, and a braid-as-the-"x" wordmark. The monograms read as generic; the orb, spark and stack read as category furniture; the marker was too loud next to the braid; the prism and aperture lost their detail below 48 px.

**Consequences:** the mark has no letter in it, so the wordmark is mandatory in every identity context. The over/under gaps vanish below ~24 px; the favicon tile therefore scales the whole braid up (86 %) instead of thickening the stroke, because thickening alone closes the weave, and the tab icon set ships an SVG, a 32 px PNG and an ICO built from it.

### 2. The wordmark is Inter Tight ExtraBold lowercase, shipped as outlines

"nexus", tracking −4.5 %, converted to paths in every committed SVG; `NexusLogo.tsx` mirrors the braid geometry for inline use. No font file is added to the repository or the bundle.

**Why:** outlines are deterministic — no font request, no silent substitution, no layout shift, and no obligation to redistribute a font binary. Lowercase and tight is the current register of software brands, and Inter Tight's x-height (54.6 % of the em) sets the proportion the braid is scaled to.

**Consequences:** the wordmark cannot be edited as text. Changing it means regenerating the outlines and replacing every lockup, which is why the masters are a small, documented set.

### 3. One violet point closes the wordmark

A 20-unit circle at font size 100, 8 units after the last glyph, sitting on the baseline, carrying the violet gradient.

**Why:** it is the smallest possible accent that still reads as deliberate, it makes the wordmark a _statement_ rather than a label, and it gives the interface a single reusable accent colour that is not the braid.

**Consequences:** the point is part of the wordmark in every lockup and is never a standalone mark; the monochrome cut renders it in the same ink as the word, so the lockup still works in one colour.

### 4. SVG masters in `assets/brand/`, rasters generated by a script

Every mark, lockup, tile and the social card is a committed SVG. The PNGs the browser serves are produced by `scripts/brand-assets.mjs` (`pnpm brand:assets`), which renders them with the Chromium already installed for Playwright, validates dimensions and colour type, and checks the manifest.

**Why:** a PNG cannot be reviewed in a diff or edited without a design tool; an SVG master can. Reusing Playwright avoids adding an image toolchain (ImageMagick, sharp, resvg).

**Consequences:** changing the identity means changing the master _and_ regenerating the committed PNGs in the same commit.

### 5. Colour comes from the tokens the app already uses

The palette is the existing indigo/violet ramp and the zinc neutrals, with four semantic signals reserved for state. No new brand colours were invented for the logo.

**Why:** the interface was already dark zinc with a violet accent; an identity with its own palette would immediately diverge from the product.

**Consequences:** the braid and point gradients are the only approved gradients, and white is used only for the braid on the tile.

## Verification

- `pnpm brand:assets` regenerates all five PNGs at the declared dimensions and colour types, copies the favicon master, and fails if `manifest.webmanifest` references a missing icon.
- The masters were rendered at 16, 24, 32, 44, 64, 192 and 512 px on light and dark surfaces; the maskable tile was checked against the 80 % safe-zone circle and the favicon at 16 px in both SVG and PNG form.
- `index.html` declares the SVG favicon with a 192 px PNG fallback and the 180 px Apple touch icon; the manifest declares `any` and `maskable` icons separately.
- The shell header, mobile header and login screen use the inline braid; the horizontal and stacked lockups are exercised in the README, the social card and the brand guide.
- `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm format:check`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm build` and the Playwright suite pass with the new component and assets.
